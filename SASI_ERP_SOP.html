1

Customer Inquiry

When a customer contacts you asking for a product, record their inquiry here.

📍 Sales → Inquiries → + New Inquiry
Fields to fill:
  • Customer name
  • Product description (e.g. Geotextile 200 GSM)
  • End use (Medical / Geotextile / Agrotextile / etc.)
  • GSM required, Width, Quantity
  • Target price, Required by date
✅ System auto-generates Inquiry Number (INQ-YYYYMMDD-001)
2

Quotation

Based on the inquiry, prepare a formal price quotation for the customer.

📍 Sales → Quotations → + New Quotation
Fields to fill:
  • Link to Inquiry (select from dropdown)
  • Unit price, Quantity
  • Valid until date, Lead time (days)
  • Payment terms, Delivery terms
  • Technical specs / notes
✅ System auto-generates Quotation Number (QT-YYYYMMDD-001)
⚠️ Once status = "Sent" — price cannot be changed

Use Print button to send PDF quotation to customer

3

Sales Order

When customer confirms the quotation, create a Sales Order to officially start the order.

📍 Sales → Create Sales Order
Fields to fill:
  • Customer, Warehouse
  • Order date, Expected delivery date
  • Items + Quantity + Unit price
✅ System auto-generates SO Number (SO-YYYYMMDD-001)
⚠️ Once "Confirmed" — SO is locked for editing
4

Delivery

After production is complete and quality passed, confirm delivery to customer.

📍 Sales → Sales Orders → Click "Deliver" button on the SO
✅ Stock automatically reduced from warehouse
✅ Stock Movement record created automatically
✅ SO status changes to "Delivered"

Use Print → Delivery Challan button to print the delivery document

5

Sales Invoice (AR)

After delivery, create an invoice to bill the customer.

📍 Sales → Invoices (AR) → + Create Invoice
Fields to fill:
  • Select the delivered Sales Order
  • Invoice date
  • Due date (payment deadline)
  • Tax amount (GST)
✅ Invoice auto-calculates total from SO amount + tax
6

Mark Invoice as Paid

When customer pays, mark the invoice as paid to close the AR cycle.

📍 Sales → Invoices → Click 💰 Pay button on the invoice
✅ Invoice status → PAID
✅ Balance due → ₹0
✅ Dashboard Outstanding AR reduces

Purchase Order (PO)

When you need to buy raw materials or items from a supplier, raise a PO.

📍 Purchasing → Create Purchase Order
Fields to fill:
  • Supplier, Warehouse (where goods will be received)
  • Order date, Expected delivery date
  • Items + Quantity + Unit price
✅ PO Number auto-generated (PO-YYYYMMDD-001)

Print PO to send to supplier

2

Goods Receipt Note (GRN)

When goods arrive from supplier, record the receipt against the PO.

📍 Purchasing → Goods Receipt (GRN) → + New GRN
Fields to fill:
  • Select Purchase Order
  • Receipt date
  • Received quantities per item
  • Notes (if any damage or shortage)
✅ Stock automatically added to warehouse
✅ Stock Movement record created
✅ PO status updates to Partial or Received
3

Verify Stock

After GRN, verify the stock has been correctly updated.

📍 Inventory → Stock List
What to check:
  • Item quantity increased correctly
  • Correct warehouse received the stock
  • Stock Movement shows the GRN entry
✅ Stock is now available for production Work Orders

Inspect the produced goods before allowing delivery to customer.

⚠️ FAIL → rework or reject batch

Technical Textile Integration
For Geotextile, Agrotextile, Industrial textile orders

🔗 When does Technical Textile connect?

After Work Order is created → Before Quality Check and Delivery → Technical specs must be defined and testing lab results recorded for all technical textile products.

1

Product Categories

Define your textile product categories (Geotextile, Agrotextile, HDPE, PP, etc.)

📍 Technical Textile → Product Categories
✅ One-time setup — reused for all orders
2

Performance Specifications

Define technical specs for each product: GSM range, tensile strength, elongation, CBR, UV resistance, etc.

📍 Technical Textile → Performance Specs
Key specs for textile:
  • GSM (grams per square meter)
  • Width (cm)
  • Tensile strength (kN/m)
  • Elongation at break (%)
  • Coating type
  • Color / finish
3

Sample Management

When customer requests samples before placing full order, manage here.

📍 Technical Textile → Sample Management
Sample workflow:
  • Prepare sample → status: Prepared
  • Send to customer → status: Sent
  • Customer approves → status: Approved
  • Then convert to full Sales Order
4

Technical Data Sheet (TDS)

Generate and maintain technical data sheets for each product — sent to customers with orders.

📍 Technical Textile → Technical Data Sheet
5

Testing Lab

Record all lab test results for produced goods before delivery.

📍 Technical Textile → Testing Lab
Tests to record:
  • GSM test result
  • Tensile test result
  • Elongation test
  • Water permeability
  • Pass/Fail status
  • Test date, Lab technician
✅ Testing PASS → proceed to QC → then Delivery
6

R&D Projects

Track new product development or custom product R&D projects.

📍 Technical Textile → R&D Projects
✅ Active R&D count shown on Dashboard

Medical Textile Integration
For surgical, wound care, and medical-grade textile orders — ISO 13485 compliant

🔗 When does Medical Textile connect?

Medical textile adds compliance, traceability, and sterility requirements on top of normal production. Every batch must be traceable, every certificate must be valid, and every CAPA must be closed before audit.

1

Regulatory Compliance

Maintain all certificates and regulatory approvals (ISO 13485, CE marking, FDA, BIS, etc.)

📍 Medical Textile → Regulatory Compliance
Track:
  • Certificate name & number
  • Issued by (certifying body)
  • Valid from / Expiry date
  • Status: Active / Expired / Pending renewal
⚠️ Dashboard alerts you 90 days before expiry!
2

Batch Traceability

Every medical product batch must be 100% traceable — raw material → production → customer.

📍 Medical Textile → Batch Traceability
Record for each batch:
  • Batch number (links to Production batch)
  • Raw material source & lot number
  • Production date
  • Customer delivered to
  • Any deviations
✅ Required for ISO 13485 audit compliance
3

Sterility Records

For sterile medical textiles (surgical drapes, gowns, wound dressings), record sterilization details.

📍 Medical Textile → Sterility Records
Record:
  • Sterilization method (ETO / Gamma / Steam)
  • Sterilization date & batch
  • Sterilization parameters
  • Sterility test result
4

CAPA Management

Corrective and Preventive Actions — record any quality failures, customer complaints, or audit findings and track resolution.

📍 Medical Textile → CAPA Management
CAPA workflow:
  • Open CAPA → record problem
  • Root cause analysis
  • Corrective action taken
  • Preventive action for future
  • Close CAPA after verification
⚠️ Open CAPAs shown as RED alert on Dashboard!
5

Shelf Life Tracking

Medical products have expiry dates. Track shelf life of all medical textile stock.

📍 Medical Textile → Shelf Life Tracking
Status types:
  • Active — within shelf life
  • Near Expiry — approaching expiry date
  • Expired — past expiry date
⚠️ Dashboard alerts for near-expiry and expired batches!
6

Audit Trail

Complete log of all actions in the system — who did what and when. Required for ISO 13485 audits.

📍 Medical Textile → Audit Trail
✅ Read-only — automatically maintained by system

1

Chart of Accounts Setup (One-time)

Define all your financial accounts. Do this once during initial setup.

📍 Finance → Chart of Accounts
Account categories:
  • Asset — Cash, Bank, Stock, Machinery
  • Liability — Loans, Accounts Payable
  • Equity — Owner Capital
  • Income — Sales Revenue, Other Income
  • Expense — Raw Material, Salary, Rent, Power
2

Journal Entry

Record every financial transaction as a journal entry (double-entry — debit must equal credit).

📍 Finance → Journal Entries → + New Entry
Example — Sales Invoice raised:
  • DEBIT: Accounts Receivable ₹1,00,000
  • CREDIT: Sales Revenue ₹1,00,000

Example — Salary paid:
  • DEBIT: Salary Expense ₹50,000
  • CREDIT: Bank Account ₹50,000
⚠️ Entry must balance — Total Debit = Total Credit
3

Post Journal Entry

After reviewing, post the entry to make it permanent and affect account balances.

📍 Finance → Journal Entries → Click "Post" button
✅ Posted entries update Trial Balance and P&L
⚠️ Posted entries are locked — cannot be edited
4

Trial Balance

View all account balances at any point in time to verify books are balanced.

📍 Finance → Trial Balance
✅ Total Debits = Total Credits → Books are balanced
💡 Finance integration tip Currently finance is manual (you create journal entries). Future enhancement: auto-create journal entries when SO is delivered or invoice is paid.
